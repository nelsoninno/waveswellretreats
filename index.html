What this is

What is the Waveswell Retreat?

The Waveswell Retreat is seven days and six nights of stand up paddle surfing in El Salvador, for SUP surfers and SUP paddlers who want to genuinely ride great waves. Spots are limited, usually six of us, and every session is filmed and reviewed together.

What sets this apart is all of it at once. The waves and the warm water are reason enough to come here, and on top of that you get high performance tools from an athlete with more than fifteen years across many water sports. Somewhere in the week we sit down one to one and work through the beliefs that are quietly limiting what your body will let you do. Those tools were never meant only for athletes.

Everything on the ground is covered: your room, every meal, all your surf sessions, the video analysis, the tours and your airport transfers. Retreats run from November to March.

Why Waveswell

Five reasons people choose this one

There are surf camps everywhere. Here is exactly what you get here that is hard to find anywhere else.

01

You train with someone who competes

I have more than fifteen years across many water sports, and I have represented El Salvador as a professional paddle surfer across nearly three Olympic cycles: World Championships in Nicaragua, Mexico, Denmark, El Salvador, Puerto Rico and France, the Pan American Games in Lima and Santiago, and gold at the Bolivarian Games. That is not a trophy shelf, it is a way of breaking a movement down so you can actually learn it.

02

El Salvador always has waves

This is not a place where you wait for a season. There are waves here all year, the water is warm enough that nobody owns a wetsuit, and the country is consistently ranked among the ten best surf destinations on the planet. You will not lose a day of your trip to a flat ocean.

03

High performance tools, one to one

Meditation, visualisation, breathing, and the way you set an objective and hold it. I learned these working with an international career development coach, Driss. We use them on your surfing first, and then you take them home and use them on everything else.

04

You are always in the video

A talented staff member is filming the action on the waves, even the wipeouts. Every day we sit down together and watch it, and you go back in the water with three clear objectives, not thirty. Groups are limited, so nobody is a face in a crowd.

05

Your week helps pay for part of a child’s year

Waveswell exists to fund Mar de Sueños, the social project I founded to put children from the Salvadoran coast through school using sport as the way in. Part of every retreat goes there. You are not buying a holiday, you are joining a positive loop.

And

You leave with something that stays

Guests come back. One of them, Sherry, has been on more than one retreat and is now a friend I have visited at her home. That is the actual measure of whether a week like this worked.

Honest fit

Who this retreat is for, and who it is not for

I would rather tell you now than have you fly here and find out. This one is easy to get wrong, so read it properly.

This is for you if

  • You have been practising stand up paddle for a while now and you stand up on the board on your own
  • You paddle surf, or you want to, on a stand up paddle board
  • You are comfortable enough in the ocean to swim by yourself
  • You are somewhere between curious and stuck, and you want to get properly better rather than just get wet
  • You like small groups and being actually known by your coach
  • You want to be part of a worldwide community of SUP surfers who love to keep improving, in the water and in life

This is not for you if

  • You have never been on a board. We encourage you to sign up for one to one SUP lessons with me first, and then come back for a retreat
  • You want shortboard or longboard surfing. We love all surfers, but in this case SUP surfers are our tribe
  • You want a party week. This is early morning, good food and a lot of water time. We want it to feel like a party every day in the water, so the night parties might need to wait

Never been on a board? That is completely fine, and it is not a no. Book one to one coaching first, get to where you can stand reliably, and come back for a retreat.

The difference

Most people ride the wave once. You will learn to ride it properly.

Here is what I see constantly. Somebody drops in, goes straight down the line, and gets off. That is it. That is the whole wave. And they are delighted, because they caught it.

What you are actually supposed to do is go down, come back up, go down, come back up, and stay with the wave. That takes reading the ocean, the right board, the right technique, and, more often than you would think, letting go of a belief about what your body can still do.

So we film you, we watch it together, and then we sit down alone and talk about how you approach things. People tend to be surprised by that conversation. After one of them, a guest and a visiting coach were still standing by a post an hour later telling me I had left them with existential doubts.

The reason all of this exists

Mar de Sueños

Waveswell did not come first. Mar de Sueños did. It is a social project I founded to reach children on the Salvadoran coast through sport, and to get them scholarships that carry them from primary school through to a degree and a real job. Every year it takes a small group, a maximum of 15 to 20 children.

Retreats are how it becomes self funding. Part of what you pay goes straight into it. The long version of the plan is a chain of hotel schools, where the hotel is where you stay and the school next door is where the young people from the programme are learning and doing their practice inside it. A virtuous circle, built on sport.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to surf already?

You need to already be able to stand up on a paddle board on your own. The retreat is not for absolute beginners, because the group moves together and a first timer would be learning while everyone else is riding. If you have never been on a board, we encourage you to sign up for one to one SUP lessons with me first, and then come back and join a retreat once you can stand reliably.

How much does the Waveswell retreat cost?

The 2026 rates are being confirmed, so write to Joss and she will quote you directly. Whatever the figure, it covers accommodation, all meals, airport transfers, every surf session, daily video analysis, the one to one coaching work, tours around El Salvador and your photos and video. The final price depends on whether you take a single, double or triple room.

What is not included in the price?

Flights, travel and medical insurance, alcoholic drinks, and board rental are not included. If you do not want to fly with your own board, a board and paddle for the whole retreat is $455, which is well below the regular daily rate. We can also connect you with someone who helps our guests with flights and insurance.

How big are the groups?

Spots are limited. The maximum is six people, and occasionally we can add one more. There is a minimum of three, and if a retreat does not reach it the retreat is cancelled and everyone is refunded. If you have already bought your flights, the booking can be converted into one to one coaching instead so your trip is not wasted.

Which airport do I fly into?

El Salvador International Airport, in El Salvador, Central America. Check this carefully when you book, because there are airports with similar names in other countries and people do get it wrong. You are picked up at the airport and driven back at the end of the week, and that transfer is included.

When is the best time to come?

Retreats run from November to March, which is the high season and lines up with winter in the north. From April to October the focus shifts to one to one coaching on your own dates. Either way there are waves, and the water is warm enough that you never need a wetsuit.

Can my partner come if they do not paddle surf?

Yes, and people do. Partners are always welcome to come and share the week with us. Write to Joss and she will put it together for you, including separate tours or private coaching while the group is in the water.

Is this stand up paddle only, or regular surfing too?

Stand up paddle surfing only. We specialise in SUP surf, not shortboard or longboard surfing, and the coaching, the video analysis and the equipment are all built around the paddle.
