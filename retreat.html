In one paragraph

What the week actually is

Seven days and six nights on the Gulf of Fonseca in La Unión, El Salvador, for a small group of adults who can already stand on a paddle board. You surf every day at whichever break the swell is favouring, someone films you from the shore without you noticing, and the group sits down together afterwards to watch it. Somewhere in the week you and Joss sit down alone for the part guests remember longest.

Between sessions there are boat trips out to the islands of the gulf, a coffee farm on a volcano, waterfalls, and long meals. From the moment you are collected at the airport to the moment you are dropped back, nothing on the ground costs you anything extra.

A day here

The rhythm of a Waveswell day

No two days are identical, because the ocean decides a lot of it. But the shape holds, and it is built so that you are in the water when the water is best and thinking about it when it is not.

  • Wake up routine

    Early, before the wind comes up. Mobility and breathing before anything gets wet.

  • Surf session

    Technical stand up paddle coaching at the break that suits the day and your level, filmed as you go.

  • Meals

    Fresh food, three times a day, cooked for the group. Dietary needs handled from the form you fill in after booking.

  • Video analysis

    The group watches the morning back together, and you go back in the water knowing how to perform better.

  • High performance tools

    The same toolkit Joss uses as a professional athlete competing worldwide.

  • Adventure and personal time

    Depending on the weather, we rest between activities: a boat trip, a coffee farm, or a hammock. Some of the week has to be yours.

  • Journaling and cool down

    Ten quiet minutes that turn out to matter more than they sound.

All of this can change according to the climate, the swell and any natural circumstance. We flow according to the ocean.

Where you surf

Four breaks and a gulf full of islands

El Salvador is consistently named among the ten best surf destinations on the planet, and the retreat is based at the quiet end of it, away from the crowds.

Home beach

Playa Blanca

Where we stay and where we surf the most. We wake up watching this beautiful beach.

Point break

One of the most beautiful waves we have with swell. During retreat season it is a very fun wave with kind sets.

Point break

Punta Mango

Like Punta Roca, but on the other side of the country. With a perfect swell, get ready for action.

And the gulf itself

La Unión sits on the Gulf of Fonseca, which El Salvador shares with Honduras and Nicaragua. Boat trips run out to the islands, past reefs, and past volcanoes on three sides. Whales pass through occasionally, which nobody can promise you and everybody remembers.

The water is warm enough all year that nobody on a Waveswell retreat has ever needed a wetsuit.

Why almost everybody rents a board

Airlines in the United States stopped accepting boards over seven feet, and a paddle board is longer and heavier than that. Retreat guests get a special rate on a SUP board and paddle for the week, and it saves you the freight, the oversize fees and the risk of your board arriving in two pieces.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to surf already?

You need to already be able to stand up on a paddle board on your own. The retreat is not for absolute beginners, because the group moves together and a first timer would be learning while everyone else is riding. If you have never been on a board, we encourage you to sign up for one to one SUP lessons with Joss first, and then come back and join a retreat once you can stand reliably.

How much does the retreat cost?

The 2026 rates are being confirmed, so write to Joss and she will quote you directly. Whatever the figure, it covers your room, every meal, airport transfers, all surf sessions, the daily video analysis, the one to one coaching work, the tours and all your photos and video. The final figure depends on whether you take a single, double or triple room.

What is not included?

Flights, travel and medical insurance, alcoholic drinks, and board rental. If you do not want to fly with your own board, SUP board and paddle rentals are available to retreat guests at a special rate.

How big are the groups?

Spots are limited. The maximum is six people, and occasionally we can add one more. There is a minimum of three, and if a retreat does not reach it the retreat is cancelled and everyone is refunded. If you have already bought flights, the booking can be converted into one to one coaching instead.

Which airport do I fly into?

El Salvador International Airport, in El Salvador, Central America. Check this carefully when you book, because there are airports with similar names in other countries. Pick up and drop off are included.

Where exactly do we surf?

The retreat is based on the Gulf of Fonseca in La Union, and the sessions move between Playa Blanca, Maculis, Las Flores and Punta Mango depending on the swell and on your level. Boat trips run out to the islands of the gulf.

When do the retreats run?

November to March, which is high season and lines up with winter in North America. It runs up to Easter week. From April to October the focus shifts to one to one coaching on your own dates.

Can my partner come if they do not paddle surf?

Yes. Partners are always welcome to come and share the tropical Latin vibes with us. Write to Joss and she will put it together for you, including separate tours or private coaching while the group is in the water.

Is this stand up paddle only, or regular surfing too?

Stand up paddle surfing only. We love all surfers, but in this case SUP surfers are our tribe, and the coaching, the video analysis and the equipment are all built around the paddle.

Is there an age limit?

No. There is no age limit in either direction. What matters is that you have been practising stand up paddle for a while, that you can stand on the board on your own, and that you can swim on your own.

What if I have a dietary restriction?

Once you have paid you receive a form covering your objectives and any dietary needs. Vegan, vegetarian and allergies are all handled, and the kitchen prepares a separate menu.

What does the health side of this actually do for me?

Through the week we work with high performance tools: meditation, visualisation, breathing, and how you set and hold an objective. They are aimed at your physical and mental performance in the water first, and they transfer straight back into everything else.

I am pregnant. Is it safe?

Every guest is welcome, and it is each visitor's responsibility to check with their doctor before booking. We would be glad to be part of your journey either way.

Are there discounts?

No standing discounts are offered. Watch the Instagram and the mailing list for occasional promotions.
